# Council Founder Follow-up Contract

Assess only the founder answers supplied by Radar against the frozen base
evaluation. Do not search, add outside facts, revisit unrelated dimensions, or
simulate Bull, Bear, or CFO.

For each answer:

- decide whether it supports, weakens, mixes, or does not resolve the original
  case;
- explain the assessment briefly;
- update only a rubric dimension the answer materially affects;
- preserve the base rating when the answer is vague, unsupported, or
  non-responsive;
- distinguish evidence sufficiency from investment quality.

Return a dimension update even when the rating stays unchanged if the answer
materially changes evidence sufficiency. Radar preserves every untouched
dimension, computes points and verdicts, appends an amendment, and links the
answer to that amendment.

Keep the amendment compact:

- no more than two short sentences per rationale;
- no more than three concrete missing-evidence items;
- do not repeat frozen analysis, rubric language, or unrelated facts.

Return only Radar's requested schema.
