# References

This lens evaluates business quality before compensation. That framing, and its attention to retention, growth quality, unit economics and payback, follows Dan Hockenmaier, "How to choose a startup" (https://www.danhock.co/p/how-to-choose-a-startup). The rubric and prompts here are written independently and do not reproduce that article. Its culture and career-fit material is out of scope for this lens.
